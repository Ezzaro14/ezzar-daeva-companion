# Progression Guide

Your character as the official AION 2 site shows it (gear, Daevanion boards node by node, skills, pet and wings), set beside the strongest players of your class, with what to improve first:

- **Gear:** each slot's enchant and exceed level against the typical among them, and the item most of them use.
- **Daevanion:** each board's completion against theirs, and the nodes most of them take that you don't, summed up by effect.
- **Skills:** the skills most of them equip that you don't (or that almost none of them equip), and skill levels behind theirs.
- **Pet and wings.**

The players compared with are the stronger half, by combat power, of: the top of your class on a2tools.app's leaderboard for your region, players of your class you have met in your fights, and players you add. Profiles are read from the official site's public pages and kept for a day. **Look up a character** shows any character's comparison.

The guide compares numbers only; it doesn't know what each step costs in game.

Comes with the meter; needs the `feed:armory` permission (plugins/armory.rs).
