# Daily Planner

A checklist of what to do each day and each week, for the best progress:
Daily Duties, Shugo Festival's three keys, Nightmare's two tries, the week's
dungeon entries before the weekly reset, Odyle Energy before it caps, the
Ascension Trial, crafting Odyle Energy with Substance Morph, and any task of
your own. Counts reset at the game's daily and weekly resets (07:00 UTC, the
weekly one on Wednesday, as Event Timers has them; both can be changed), and
a notice comes up a few hours before a reset while something is left. Kept
per character.
