# Combat Alerts

- **Loss of control.** While you are stunned, asleep, feared, knocked down, airborne, rooted, frozen, petrified, charmed, transformed or silenced, a notice shows where you put it (just above your character works best) with what holds you and the time left, and a sound plays. Pick which kinds show.
- **Party debuffs.** When enemies put the same debuff on at least N members of your party (you included) at once, an alert shows with how many have it and a sound plays, to time a cleanse such as for Heal Block. Watch any enemy debuff, or only those you list; debuffs seen on your party are listed to pick from.

A debuff counts as an enemy's when neither you nor a party member applied it. The kind of a loss of control is read from the game's icon for it.

Comes with the meter; needs the `feed:combat` permission (plugins/bundled.rs).
