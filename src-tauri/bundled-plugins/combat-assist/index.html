Your skill cooldowns and buffs as icons over the game, so you can see what's ready without looking at your hotbar.

Waiting for the game…

Set up a bar in three steps

  1. Use your skills in game. Every skill you cast shows up under Your skills. The app reads them from the game as you play; nothing to type.
  2. Click Add on the skills and buffs you want to watch. They go on the bar you've picked below.
  3. Put the bar where you want it on screen. Drag it into place, then press Done. Every bar and alert can be moved the same way.
How does it know my cooldowns?

The game never says when a skill comes back, so the app learns each cooldown from how you use it: cast a skill, then cast it again the moment it's ready, twice, and the bar knows. Until then the icon shows a ?, or a first guess marked ~.

A skill whose hit lands after the cast (a delayed explosion) is counted as one cast. If a cooldown still comes out wrong, press Re-learn next to it, or type the seconds.

If a skill was cast sooner than usual (something shortened its cooldown), the bar marks it ▼ and keeps the normal time.

Know the cooldown already? Type the seconds next to the skill under On this bar.
