# Fight Records

**Personal bests.** Your best DPS on each boss (by dungeon and difficulty), per character, from the fights the meter saves to History, with a notice and a sound when you beat it.

**Training.** Timed runs on a training dummy: pick 30 seconds, 1, 2, 3 or 5 minutes, or until you stop, press Start (here or on the training widget), and hit a dummy: the clock starts at your first hit, and only your own damage on dummies counts. Each run is kept with its DPS, hits, critical, back attack, perfect and double rates and every skill; name it, group it, add notes, and compare two runs side by side, skill by skill.

Comes with the meter; needs the `feed:combat` permission (plugins/bundled.rs).
